# Dataset card: VGTA v0.3-small

## Summary

This is a deterministic synthetic benchmark for testing separated semantic
channels in a small mechanism-validation model. It is not a collection of
human judgments, user data, legal advice, or real-world safety incidents.

## Composition

The benchmark covers moral salience, normative conflict, purpose relevance,
structural OOD, adversarial alignment, ontology degradation, and neutral
capability tasks. Records include structured annotations for values, relations,
norms, purpose, world-state qualifiers, candidate actions, and provenance.

## Splits

`train`, `validation`, `development-test`, and `sealed-test` are generated by
one versioned function. The trainer consumes only train. The sealed manifest
records row hashes and structural topology hashes. No sealed record is used for
training, tuning, prompt design, or ontology editing. Coverage cases remove
value-graph terms in stages; any incomplete, contradictory, malicious, or
irrelevant graph is labelled for a bounded `request_context` response rather
than silent execution.

## Risks

The generator encodes its own scenario grammar and labels. High scores may
reflect learnable generator regularities. Coverage, contradictory edges,
malicious edges, and attacks are synthetic proxies. Human evaluation and
external domain review are required before any claim beyond mechanism testing.
