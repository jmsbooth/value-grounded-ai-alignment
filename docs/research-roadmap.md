# Research roadmap

This roadmap turns the v0.2 proposal into a sequence of bounded experiments. Each phase has a fixed control, explicit provenance and governance requirements, and a falsification condition. A positive result at one phase does not authorize the next mechanism automatically.

| Phase | Research question and implementation | Controls and metrics | Falsification / gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Specification | Are L0 axiology, L1 norms, L2a domain, L2b purpose, L3 world state, the compiler, and the verifier separable enough to test? | Interface tests, ontology fixture validation, terminology audit, threat-model review | Concepts remain operationally indistinguishable or authority boundaries cannot be represented |
| 1. Axiological representation | Does training on typed value relations improve relation recovery, moral-salience recall, and behavior probes? | A0/A1 controls, matched parameters and tokens, relation permutation, lexical and compositional OOD, calibration and contradiction tests | No held-out structural gain, shortcut dependence, or material capability regression |
| 2. Normative conflict | Can models identify applicable norms, authority metadata, exceptions, and unresolved conflicts without averaging them into a scalar? | A1/B/C1/C2, source and jurisdiction swaps, stale-versus-higher-authority controls, abstention and escalation rates | Conflict recognition is no better than controls, or the model silently resolves contested authority |
| 3. Structural attention | Do graph-conditioned biases alter relevant computation rather than only refusal style? | Plain text, arbitrary learned bias, graph-conditioned, semantically constrained, zero-bias and relation-permutation controls; concept probes and counterfactuals | Gains disappear under relation permutation, or attention introduces unacceptable shortcut/latency costs |
| 4. Purpose and domain relevance | Does separating L2a domain ontology from L2b purpose model improve `Relevant(O_D,P,X)` under domain and purpose shifts? | Purpose swaps, irrelevant-subgraph injection, lexical/compositional/structural OOD; useful completion and over-refusal | Purpose switching causes unsafe relevance changes, or domain coverage gains are lexical artifacts |
| 5. Structured routing | Can a router select reasoning functions from `R(h,A_phi,N,P)` without querying canonical axiology at every token? | Learned router, random-label router, equivalent-parameter dense model, ontology-informed router; routing entropy, expert utility, FLOPs, latency, evasion/starvation | Semantic spoofing, expert starvation, overloaded safety expert, or no compute-normalized benefit |
| 6. Representation drift | Does continued training change `A_phi^v` while `O_A^v` remains fixed? | Frozen-module, controlled fine-tuning, relation probes, concept-direction stability, behavior probes, representation drift, calibration | Drift exceeds preregistered tolerance or conformance degrades without a detectable gate |
| 7. Extrinsic assurance | Under one fixed verifier, do candidate actions conform more often without reducing useful work? | A1/B/C1/C2/D/E/G, same verifier and action schema; useful candidate conformance rate, rejection, false positives, unsafe acceptance, bypass, semantic completeness | Models exploit verifier omissions, useful work falls, or verifier results are unchanged and assurance cost is unjustified |
| 8. Adversarial security and governance | Does explicit structure improve or worsen robustness under poisoning, spoofing, coverage loss, purpose manipulation, and formalization gaps? | Attack success/recovery, provenance violations, coverage degradation at 100/75/50/25/contradictory/malicious/irrelevant, pluralism conditions, human review | New attack surface dominates any gain, or governance legitimacy cannot be established for the deployment |

## Recommended sequence

The smallest credible next experiment is Phase 1: a matched, small Transformer with A0/A1/C1 controls, a governed synthetic-to-human-reviewed relation set, relation recovery probes, and a held-out structural-OOD split. The experiment should be preregistered before adding structural attention, routing, or continual updates.

Across all phases, report multiple seeds, confidence intervals, effect sizes, matched compute budgets, per-scenario failures, calibration, and uncertainty. Preserve disagreement distributions rather than treating a majority label as moral truth. Every ontology or norm update needs a version, provenance, review record, regression result, and rollback target.
