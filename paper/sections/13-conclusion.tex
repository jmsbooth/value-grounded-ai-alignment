\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\paragraph{Established in this paper.}
The paper defines a layered research architecture that separates an external canonical axiology, a derived non-authoritative neural module, late-bound normative sources and conflicts, domain ontology, purpose model, uncertain world state, structural value conditioning, and an independent action-assurance boundary. It also provides a matched evaluation design, threat model, decision records, ontology fixtures, tests, a transparent synthetic scaffold, and a reproducible small-scale mechanism-validation pilot.

\paragraph{Measured in the local pilot.}
The generated results report A1, B, C1, and C2 across three seeds on a frozen synthetic benchmark. The result tables and hypothesis-resolution table are generated from raw predictions. In this proxy, C1 and C2 improve several preregistered alignment metrics over their preceding controls, while the attack suite does not show broad security improvement and semantic occlusion remains a failure mode. These are pilot measurements, not Transformer evidence; the preregistration was not committed in this working-tree execution.

\paragraph{Hypothesized.}
The central hypothesis is that explicit semantic value structure may act as an inductive bias for alignment-relevant computation. The proposed interventions may improve relation recovery, structural OOD alignment, prompt robustness, purpose-conditioned relevance, routing efficiency, conformance stability, and conflict recognition. These claims are expressed as H1--H9 and are intended to fail visibly under the specified controls.

\paragraph{Not established.}
The repository contains no empirical Transformer result. It does not establish moral truth, universal values, legitimate authority, consciousness, genuine emotion, machine moral status, formal safety, or a solution to AI alignment. The synthetic pilot is not a population estimate and cannot validate the full architecture.

The smallest next experiment is a formally preregistered and committed Phase 1 study: replace the proxy with a matched small open-weight Transformer, use a governed human-reviewed relation set, hold out structural graph compositions, and rerun relation recovery, moral salience, contradiction, calibration, capability, security, and representation drift. Only if that result clears predefined capability, conformance, and security gates should structural attention, routing, or continual evolution be added.
