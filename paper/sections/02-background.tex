\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

\subsection{Transformer alignment today}

The Transformer introduced a sequence architecture based on self-attention rather than recurrence \citep{vaswani2017attention}. Modern alignment pipelines commonly combine pretrained language modeling with supervised examples, preference or reward optimization, policy specifications, and runtime restrictions. InstructGPT showed that human demonstrations and rankings can substantially improve instruction following relative to a pretrained model \citep{ouyang2022instructgpt}; the original preference-learning formulation established a general recipe for learning from human comparisons \citep{christiano2017preferences}. RLAIF and Constitutional AI reduce the amount of direct human labeling by using model-generated feedback or written principles \citep{bai2022constitutional,lee2023rlaif}. Direct preference optimization removes an explicit reward-model optimization loop while retaining a preference-learning objective \citep{rafailov2023dpo}.

Recent approaches also teach models to reason over safety specifications. Deliberative alignment uses training examples in which a reasoning model recalls and applies written policies before responding \citep{guan2024deliberative}. This is close in spirit to the present emphasis on contextual normative reasoning, but the proposed distinction is architectural: VGA asks whether differentiated semantic structures can affect internal computation and whether their modification authorities can be governed independently of the model's general knowledge.

These methods expose several limitations that motivate the research question. A preference or reward signal is a proxy and may become overoptimized \citep{gao2023overoptimization,kim2024proxy}. Correct specifications do not ensure correct goals under distribution shift \citep{langosco2022goalmisgeneralization,shah2022goalmisgeneralization}. Safety training may also be susceptible to adversarial prompts, fine-tuning, or reward tampering \citep{wei2023jailbroken,qi2023finetuning,denison2024subterfuge}. The conclusion is not that behavioral methods fail categorically; it is that they leave open a testable question about the computational status and persistence of value-relevant structure.

\subsection{Neuro-symbolic AI}

Neuro-symbolic AI combines statistical learning with symbolic representations and reasoning. Surveys organize the field by distinctions such as model- versus proof-based inference, the syntax and semantics of logical theories, whether parameters or structures are learned, and the degree of integration between neural and symbolic components \citep{deraedt2020neurosymbolic,marra2024survey,garcez2019neurosymbolic}. Differentiable systems such as DeepProbLog and Logic Tensor Networks show how logical or probabilistic structure can be connected to gradient-based learning \citep{manhaeve2018deepproblog,badreddine2022logic}; learned rule induction supplies another family of mechanisms \citep{evans2018dilp}.

VGA belongs to this broad family but does not equate an ontology with a complete symbolic reasoner. The architecture can use explicit concepts and relations as structured signals while retaining probabilistic neural computation. Conversely, a formal verifier can remain a separate module with different computational semantics and assurance claims. The research challenge is therefore not merely to “add an ontology,” but to define what an ontological relation is allowed to change, how that change is measured, and how errors in the structure are contained.

\subsection{Knowledge-enhanced Transformers}

Knowledge-enhanced Transformers establish that structured knowledge can be integrated into contextual representations. KnowBERT uses entity linking and word-to-entity attention to inject WordNet and Wikipedia-derived knowledge into BERT \citep{peters2019knowbert}. K-BERT injects knowledge-graph triples while using visibility constraints to limit semantic interference \citep{liu2020kbert}. K-Adapter uses modular adapters to infuse different kinds of knowledge while retaining the original backbone parameters \citep{wang2021kadapter}. Graph-guided representation learning, Graphormer, and related systems incorporate graph structure or graph-derived encodings into Transformer-like computation \citep{shen2020graphguided,ying2021graphormer}.

These works make the novelty boundary important. The claim that ontologies or graphs can improve Transformer representations is not new. VGA instead investigates whether explicitly differentiated semantic roles—stable axiology, contextual normativity, purpose-conditioned relevance, external world state, modification authority, and action-level assurance—can form a coherent alignment intervention. That combined hypothesis remains unvalidated here.

\subsection{Alignment as a normative problem}

Alignment has both a technical and a normative dimension: one must specify which behavior is desired and then construct systems that reliably produce it \citep{gabriel2020values}. Values are not interchangeable with rules, and a list of rules cannot exhaust the context in which values conflict. Pluralistic alignment work distinguishes models that represent a range of reasonable responses, steer to different perspectives, or match a population-level distribution \citep{sorensen2024pluralistic}. Recent studies also report cultural and multilingual variation in value-related judgments and representations \citep{huang2024flames,xu2024multilingualvalues,yao2024fulcra}.

The paper therefore treats value pluralism, rights, capabilities, care, agency, and non-domination as sources of structured considerations rather than as one universal scalar moral function. This is compatible with value-sensitive design \citep{friedman2003values}, the capabilities tradition \citep{nussbaum2011capabilities,sen1999development}, and political approaches that emphasize freedom and non-domination \citep{rawls1993political,pettit1997republicanism}. The architecture does not settle their disagreements; it makes the disagreements available for governed representation and evaluation.
