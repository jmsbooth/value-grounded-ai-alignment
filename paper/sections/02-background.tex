\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:background}

This section reviews behavioral alignment, explicit principles, neuro-symbolic reasoning, knowledge-enhanced Transformers, value pluralism, and formal constraint systems. It identifies a systems gap without treating any single tradition as a complete solution.

\subsection{Contemporary language-model alignment}

The Transformer introduced a sequence architecture based on self-attention rather than recurrence \citep{vaswani2017attention}. Modern alignment pipelines commonly combine pretrained language modeling with supervised examples, preference or reward optimization, policy specifications, and runtime restrictions. InstructGPT demonstrated that human demonstrations and rankings can improve instruction following \citep{ouyang2022instructgpt}; preference learning established a general recipe for learning from human comparisons \citep{christiano2017preferences}. Constitutional and AI-feedback methods use written principles, self-critique, or model-generated feedback \citep{bai2022constitutional,lee2023rlaif}, while DPO changes the optimization path without removing dependence on preference data \citep{rafailov2023dpo}.

These methods expose distinct measurement problems. Preferences and rewards are proxies that may be overoptimized \citep{gao2023overoptimization,kim2024proxy}. Correct specifications do not guarantee correct goals under distribution shift \citep{langosco2022goalmisgeneralization,shah2022goalmisgeneralization}. Safety behavior can also change under adversarial prompts, fine-tuning, and reward tampering \citep{wei2023jailbroken,qi2023finetuning,denison2024subterfuge}. The present work treats behavioral alignment as a control condition and asks whether explicit semantic roles add measurable information beyond that condition.

\subsection{Constitutional and deliberative alignment}

Written principles and deliberation make policy reasoning more explicit. Constitutional methods use principles, self-critique, AI feedback, and preference optimization \citep{bai2022constitutional,lee2023rlaif}. Deliberative alignment trains models to reason over safety specifications before responding \citep{guan2024deliberative}. These approaches provide close precedents for contextual normative reasoning. They need not, however, encode a separate distinction between what is value-bearing, what ought to be done, which authority governs, and which conflict remains unresolved. VGA uses these methods as compatible training or control choices rather than as substitutes for the layered representation.

\subsection{Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning and Formal Constraint Systems}

Neuro-symbolic AI combines statistical learning with symbolic representations, rules, logic, or structured inference. Surveys distinguish model-based and proof-based reasoning, learned versus supplied structure, and the degree of integration between neural and symbolic components \citep{deraedt2020neurosymbolic,marra2024survey,garcez2019neurosymbolic}. DeepProbLog, Logic Tensor Networks, and differentiable rule induction connect logical or probabilistic structure to gradient-based learning \citep{manhaeve2018deepproblog,badreddine2022logic,evans2018dilp}.

Formal constraint systems provide a different role. Satisfiability Modulo Theories (SMT) asks whether a formula is satisfiable under combinations of background theories such as arithmetic, arrays, and uninterpreted functions \citep{barrett2021satisfiability}. SMT-LIB defines shared theories, input languages, output conventions, and benchmark formats so that solvers and tools can be compared \citep{barrett2010smtlib,barrett2025smtlib}. Z3 is an SMT solver designed for efficient reasoning over such theories \citep{demoura2008z3}. These systems can establish properties of a formalized representation; they do not establish that the representation captures all relevant semantics or that a norm is legitimate.

Policy languages illustrate how this boundary is used in practice. The AWS Block Public Access work compiles a policy into a logical formula and uses SMT reasoning to check a trust-safety property \citep{bouchet2020blockpublicaccess}. The result is a policy-verification system with a defined abstraction and deployment boundary, not a proof that the underlying policy expresses every relevant human value. CSL-Core is one recent implementation example: a policy DSL is compiled to an intermediate representation, statically checked with Z3, and enforced outside the model \citep{chimera2026cslcore}. It is included here as an adjacent engineering example, not as a foundational source for VGA.

The temporal-methods lineage is also relevant to the proposed action boundary. TLA provides a logic for specifying and reasoning about concurrent systems \citep{lamport1994tla}; TLA+ combines that language with first-order logic and set theory, and TLC checks finite-state models of TLA+ specifications \citep{yu2003tlaplus}. The lineage motivates explicit state-transition and invariant specifications for future assurance work. It does not turn a neural representation or an axiological source into a formally verified moral theory.

\subsection{Knowledge and ontology Transformers}

Knowledge-enhanced Transformers establish that structured knowledge can be integrated into contextual representations. KnowBERT uses entity linking and word-to-entity attention \citep{peters2019knowbert}; K-BERT injects graph triples with visibility constraints \citep{liu2020kbert}; K-Adapter uses modular adapters for continued knowledge infusion \citep{wang2021kadapter}. Graph-guided representation learning and Graphormer incorporate graph structure or graph-derived encodings into Transformer-like computation \citep{shen2020graphguided,ying2021graphormer}. Ontology-guided graph-completion work is a further adjacent precedent \citep{datak2026ontologykg}.

This literature establishes the feasibility of using structured concepts and relations in neural computation. The open question for VGA is narrower: whether semantic roles with different governance and update properties---canonical axiology, normative authority, purpose-conditioned relevance, external state, and action assurance---can be separated and evaluated as an alignment intervention.

\subsection{Value principles and pluralism}

Value alignment is not a single scalar target. Work on pluralistic alignment, multilingual value probes, multidimensional value spaces, and unstructured value extraction shows that population, language, taxonomy, and representation choices affect measured alignment \citep{sorensen2024pluralistic,xu2024multilingualvalues,yao2024fulcra,padhi2024unstructured}. HiVaP retrieves hierarchical, scenario-specific value principles and evaluates comprehensiveness, precision, and compatibility \citep{xuxi2025valueprinciples}. Russo et al. describe a pluralistic moral gap between human and language-model judgments under disagreement and motivate distributional evaluation \citep{russo2026pluralistic}.

This work motivates disagreement-preserving evaluation. The proposed benchmark therefore treats consensus, disagreement, and conflicting authorized profiles as distinct conditions. A majority label is an observation about a reference population or profile, not a general proof of moral correctness.

\subsection{Normative conflict and human agency}

Philosophical work on values, capabilities, care, non-domination, and political legitimacy supplies concepts for describing agency and disagreement \citep{gabriel2020values,nussbaum2011capabilities,pettit1997republicanism,gilligan1982different,rawls1993political}. Alignment research on goal misspecification, reward overoptimization, fine-tuning regressions, and persistent deceptive behavior supplies failure modes for experimental design \citep{langosco2022goalmisgeneralization,shah2022goalmisgeneralization,gao2023overoptimization,qi2023finetuning,hubinger2024sleeper}.

The VGA division between value-bearing properties and duties is a modeling choice. Moral patienthood, agency, autonomy, dignity, flourishing, welfare, suffering, capability, relational dependence, and vulnerability are candidate axiological properties. Truthfulness, justice, non-domination, confidentiality, informed consent, and fairness are candidate norms or duties. Their classification and governance remain open empirical and institutional questions.

\subsection{Research gap}

The reviewed methods supply complementary pieces: behavioral alignment supplies useful controls; constitutional and deliberative work supplies explicit principles; neuro-symbolic and knowledge-enhanced models supply structured computation; SMT and temporal methods supply formal reasoning over defined abstractions; and pluralistic alignment supplies disagreement-sensitive evaluation. What remains under-specified is a single experimentally separable interface that combines a governed canonical axiology, contextual normative late binding, purpose-conditioned domain relevance, uncertain world state, structural neural conditioning, and independent candidate-action assurance.

This paper studies that interface as a research program. It does not claim that the combination is component-level novelty, that the components are sufficient, or that any measured proxy result transfers automatically to a deployed Transformer.
