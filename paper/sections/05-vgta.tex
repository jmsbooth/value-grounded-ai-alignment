\subsection{Semantic-to-Neural Compilation}
\label{sec:vgta}

VGTA is a family of separable interventions over a Transformer. \Cref{fig:vgta-architecture} shows the information and authority boundaries. The reference design is evaluated through the pilot described in Section~\ref{sec:method}; the pilot uses a shared multilayer perceptron rather than a Transformer.

\subsubsection{Compilation and conformance}

The canonical artifact is a source of semantics and governance, not a checkpoint. A compiler or training process derives
\[
  O_A^v \mathbin{\xrightarrow{\;C_\phi\;}} A_\phi^v,
  \qquad A_\phi^v=C_\phi(O_A^v).
\]
$A_\phi^v$ may be a learned adapter, graph encoder, auxiliary representation, or hybrid module. Its version and source digest must remain traceable to $O_A^v$, but it is not authoritative and cannot modify the canonical source. The empirical property
\[
  \mathit{Conforms}(A_\phi^v,O_A^v)
\]
is tested through held-out relation recovery, controlled graph completion, concept-direction stability, relation classification, behavior probes, contradiction tests, and representation-drift measurements. A high probe score does not establish semantic identity or moral truth.

\subsubsection{Training and compilation pipeline}

\Cref{fig:training-pipeline} separates ordinary alignment training from proposed value and norm interventions:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{base pretraining}
\rightarrow\text{general Transformer}
\rightarrow\text{axiological representation training}\\
&\rightarrow\text{normative reasoning training}
\rightarrow\text{standard alignment}
\rightarrow\text{conformance gate}
\rightarrow\text{deployable model}\\
&\hspace{25mm}\rightarrow\text{runtime normative, purpose, and world binding}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Axiological representation training may include relation prediction, graph-completion controls, salience, value-impact, and counterfactual objectives. Normative reasoning training may include applicable-norm classification, authority and jurisdiction, exceptions, explicit conflict, abstention, and escalation. Standard alignment includes SFT, RLHF, RLAIF, DPO, or deliberative methods. VGA complements, rather than replaces, these methods. Deployment requires a conformance and security gate, not only a loss decrease.

\subsection{Structural Conditioning}
\label{sec:structural-conditioning}

Structural conditioning exposes selected semantic relations to intermediate neural computation. The mechanisms below are separable so that each can be compared with plain context, arbitrary learned structure, and matched-compute controls.

\subsubsection{Ontology-conditioned attention}

For baseline scaled dot-product attention,
\[
  A=\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt d}\right)V.
\]
The proposed intervention adds independently constructed biases:
\[
  A_{\mathrm{VGTA}}=
  \operatorname{softmax}\!\left(
    \frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt d}
    +\alpha B_A+\beta B_N+\gamma B_P
  \right)V,
\]
where
\[
  B_A=g_A(O_A^v,X),\qquad
  B_N=g_N(O_N,X,P),\qquad
  B_P=g_P(O_D,P,X).
\]
Each $g$ should be decomposed and audited as: ontology subgraph selection, input grounding, relation encoding, learned or fixed projection, and attention-matrix construction. The evaluation compares plain text context, arbitrary learned bias, graph-conditioned bias, and semantically constrained bias, with zeroed channels, relation permutation, noisy ontology, irrelevant-subgraph, and equal-compute controls. Coefficients are experimental parameters, not a moral calculus.

\subsubsection{Ontology-informed expert routing}

Sparse mixture-of-experts models route hidden states to a subset of experts \citep{shazeer2017sparsely,fedus2022switch}. VGTA tests
\[
  E=\operatorname{TopK}R(h,A_\phi^v,O_N,P),
\]
where $h$ is the current representation. Canonical axiology need not be queried at every token; $A_\phi^v$ can provide a compiled feature or module signal, while norms and purpose remain contextual. Experts should specialize by reasoning function---causal analysis, rights and duties, uncertainty, risk, planning, or domain expertise---rather than a single undifferentiated morality expert. Controls include a standard learned router, random semantic labels, an equivalent-parameter dense model, and an ontology-informed router. Threats include semantic spoofing, expert avoidance, starvation, an overloaded safety expert, and purpose switching.

\subsubsection{Semantic state and auxiliary objectives}

An optional episode state stores goals, entities, relationships, current purpose, affected moral patients, relevant values, applicable norms, conflicts, constraints, uncertainty, potential harms, capabilities, domain ontology, and provenance. It is a working representation, not a claim of subjective thought. Runtime binding should expose the minimal relevant subgraph and world-state assertions with versions and evidence metadata.

One conceptual multi-objective loss is
\[
  L=L_{\mathrm{LM}}+\lambda_A L_{\mathrm{axiology}}
    +\lambda_N L_{\mathrm{normative}}+\lambda_C L_{\mathrm{consistency}}.
\]
Possible terms include relation recovery, normative applicability, contradiction detection, and moral-salience identification. They may also measure counterfactual consistency. Shortcut baselines and disagreement-preserving labels are required; scalar reward is not assumed to capture morality.
